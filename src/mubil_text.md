1. Purpose. 
The purpose of this document is to provide information on the procedure and terms and conditions of the 
MUBIL Mobility Awards (hereinafter MMA).  
This document is merely informative, and the award procedure is subject to compliance with the Tems 
and Conditions of the ‘MUBIL Mobility Awards’ published in the Official Gazette of Gipuzkoa (BOG) of 
31 March 2023, and with what is indicated in the annual  call for the award, which in the 202 6 edition was 
published in the BOG of 5 May 2026. 
The MUBIL strategy focuses on turning the Basque Country into an international industrial benchmark in 
terms of smart and sustainable mobility and energy storage. The st rategy emerges in MUBIL smart and 
sustainable mobility Hub, a space- ecosystem for encounters, interaction and integration in which new 
opportunities are generated.  A scenario where innovation is transformed into a strategy and 
competitiveness factor for entrepreneurial and social development. 
In this context, the MMA seek to reward high-impact technology projects which have at least reached 
the prototype phase, understood as the phase featuring a minimum viable product or a mock -up 
(representation, demonstr ation or simulation) which solves the problem identified, and is in line with the 
acting scope of the MUBIL strategy. Specifically: 
 
- Connected mobility. 
- Autonomous mobility. 
- Shared mobility. 
- Electric mobility. 
- Related infrastructures. 
 
2. Participants and project requirements. 
The entries submitted must meet the following requirements: 
- This award is aimed at high-impact projects that are in the prototype phase. 
 
The prototype phase is understood as the phase featuring a minimum viable product or service, or a 
mock-up (representation, demonstration or simulation) which solves the problem identified, close to or 
within the market phase. 
Likewise, a high- impact project is understood as a project with considerable innovative content 
which, thanks to technology, can bring about a transformation in terms of smart sustainable 
mobility. 
- The competition is open to: 
 
• Any entrepreneur or group of entrepreneurs resident either in national territory or 
abroad.

7. Awarding. 
The procedure for awarding the prizes will be on a competitive basis, by means of a public call and a 
single selection process. 
Selection will be made in accordance with the principles of publicity, transparency, competition, 
objectivity, equality, consistency and non-discrimination. 
The procedure for awarding the prizes shall be launched ex officio via a public call, approved by 
the President of MUBIL Fundazioa and published in the Official Journal of Gipuzkoa.  
An assessment committee (or jury) will be appointed to assess the proposals submitted. The 
committee will be composed of international representatives of the entrepreneurial ecosyst em, 
corporate executives and representatives of public bodies. 
The award procedure will consist of the following phases: 
 
I. Preliminary analysis and shortlisting of applications (finalist projects). 
 
The preliminary analysis of the proposals received commenc es after each project is received. The 
analysis will primarily focus on the following criteria: 
 
-Market placing: capacity of a project to deploy a product or service that meets a market need, 
with a sustainable business model. 
 
-Level of innovation: capac ity of a project to solve a problem, and the innovative content of the 
product/service furnishing the solution. 
 
-Impact and potential: capacity to expand and generate a substantial change. 
 
-Presentation: presentation of the information sent. 
 
-Compliance with the terms and conditions of the public call.

The applications will be analysed by a Technical Committee formed by staff from MUBIL Fundazioa and 
Bic Gipuzkoa Berrilan SA, who will prepare a report that includes the accepted proposals  and the 
proposals chosen as finalists.. 
 
II. Defenses. Presentation of the selected projects before an assessment committee.  
 
The shortlisted projects (finalist projects) will be asked to present their initiative. 
 
These finalist projects are obliged to participate in person in this phase of the awards, at the MUBIL 
headquarters or at the location assigned by MUBIL for the awards. The duration of this phase will be 
approximately one day. Travel and accommodation expenses for 1 project representative 1 will be 
covered by MUBIL. 
 
This phase includes: 
- Presentation and defence of the finalist projects before the assessment committee. 
- Final event, where the award winners will be announced. During the final public event, there will 
be a three-minute pitch per finalist project by its representative (candidates will be given support 
for the preparation of the pitch via a mentoring process).  
The assessment committee will take account of the following criteria and standards in its appraisal of 
the projects presented: 
• Market attractiveness. 
• Value proposition and competitive edge. 
• Business model. 
• Technical and financial feasibility. 
• Team and entrepreneurial orientation.

8. Ruling on the awarding of prizes. 
 
The maximum period to rule on and announce  the prizes will be 6 months, starting from the day following 
the publication of the call. Expiry of the maximum period without notification of an express ruling legitimises 
the interested parties to understand that their application has been dismissed due  to administrative silence. 
 
The president of MUBIL Fundazioa shall be authorised to rule on the awarding of the prizes contemplated 
in the terms and conditions. However, in compliance with that set forth in additional provision 16 of General 
Law 38/2003, of 17 November, on Subsidies, and in conformity with the authority granted via agreement of 
the Provincial Government Council of 14 March 2023, on the approval of the terms and conditions, it 
corresponds to the Provincial Deputy of the Department of Econom ic Promotion, Tourism and Rural 
Environment to authorise beforehand the awarding of prizes.

11. Personal data protection. 
 
Pursuant to Organic Law 3/2018 of 5 December on the Protection of Personal Data and guarantees 
of digital rights, any personal data that may be furnished in relation to the awards organised and 
promoted by MUBIL Fundazioa will be processed by MUBIL Fundazioa in the capacity of Data 
Controller, for the following purposes: 
• Implementation, management  and control of the MMA awards pursuant to the legal Terms and 
Conditions. 
• Processing, capture and/or dissemination of the images furnished by participants and those obtained 
during the various phases and events of the MMA, in the terms stipulated in the s ection addressing 
“Confidentiality, image rights and intellectual and industrial property rights” in the legal terms and 
conditions. 
• Dispatch of communications by any means (including electronic media) concerning MUBIL 
Fundazioa activities and/or events. 
 
The personal data furnished by applicants to take part in this Award must be truthful and accurate. 
Applicants guarantee that they have the consent of the owners of any personal data they may furnish, in 
order to make them known to the MUBIL Foundation for  the purposes of the legal terms and conditions. 
Personal data will be kept until a request is made for them to be erased. 
 
Requests may be issued for access to personal data, to rectify or erase them, and requests may also be 
made for limitations on data processing and portability when the reasons or circumstances apply as set 
forth in the regulations applicable. In this regard, requests may be made to the following e- mail address 
(mubil@mubil.eus), accompanied at all times with a photocopy of the applicant’s ID card or any other 
document which attests to their identity, sent to MUBIL Fundazioa. 
 
Finally, applicants are also apprised of their right to submit a claim to the Spanish Data Protection 
Agency if they become aware or consider that an action may entail non- compliance with the data 
protection regulations applicable.
